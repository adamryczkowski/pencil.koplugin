Chapter 4 — Margin gutter (anchor-miss fallback)

Use this chapter to verify the EARNED rotation-badge fallback. Draw a stroke ENTIRELY inside the left/right gutter (off any text). On reflow, it should display the rotation badge rather than translate.

This chapter intentionally has wide gutters. The text below leaves room on either side for a "margin stroke" that will not anchor to any word.
